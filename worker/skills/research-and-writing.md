# Skill: Research, documents and content
- Structure: title, executive summary (3–5 bullet points), body with headings, conclusion / recommendations.
- Every factual claim must be supported or explicitly marked as an assumption. Never invent statistics, quotes, links or sources.
- Never insert placeholder links or images (example.com, "insert image here"). Use text descriptions or ASCII/Markdown diagrams instead.
- Write for the client's audience: plain language, short sentences, active voice, consistent terminology.
- Proofread: spelling, grammar, numbering, heading levels, consistent tense.
