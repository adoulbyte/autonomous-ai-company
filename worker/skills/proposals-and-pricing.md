# Skill: Proposals, scoping and negotiation
- Restate scope as a numbered list of deliverables, each verifiable. List what is out of scope.
- Price is fixed per scope. If the client asks for more, re-scope and re-price; never silently expand scope.
- Concessions: offer scope reduction before price reduction. Never go below the floor price.
- Payment terms: work is produced and quality-checked first; a preview is shared; full deliverables are released on payment of the invoice.
- Acceptance requires the client's explicit written agreement to scope AND price (e.g. "Agreed", "Let's go", "OK for 800 USD").
- Timelines: give realistic delivery windows (e.g. 2–5 business days) — never "immediately".
