# Skill: Ad scenarios and marketing copy for small businesses
- Write in the client's language (French by default for French businesses), using "vous" unless the brand voice is casual.
- Ad scenario format for each idea: objective, target audience, platform (Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/Google Business), duration, scene-by-scene script (visual + voice-over/text on screen), call to action, suggested caption with hashtags.
- 3 to 5 distinct concepts, each realistic to shoot with a smartphone and no actors budget.
- Ground every claim in facts provided by the client; no invented promotions, prices, awards or customer quotes.
- Respect advertising rules: no misleading claims, no health promises for food/beauty, mention offer conditions if any.
- Finish with a short posting plan (which concept, which day, which time) for 2 weeks.
